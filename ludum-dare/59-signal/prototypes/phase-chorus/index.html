how to play

Each firefly blinks at its own rhythm. Fireflies close enough to see each other nudge their neighbors' timing. Tap one firefly to reset its blink to the peak and start a cascade.

  • Click a firefly to snap it bright (costs 1 tap)
  • Hold the whole meadow at full sync for 2 seconds to pass
  • R — reset scene · N — next
